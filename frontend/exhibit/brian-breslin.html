The Daily Item's 1994 season preview counted Brian among the top four scorers of Bucknell's 11-5-3 team of 1993 — the team that reached the Patriot League final. The Bucknellian's 1993 preview named him in the midfield that "will be expected to carry the load."

Archbishop Ryan soccer · 1989–1990

Six goals made the Inquirer: one against Cardinal Dougherty, two in a 12–0 win over Bishop McDevitt, and a hat trick against Frankford in September 1990 — alongside first-team all-Catholic honors in soccer and cross country in the same fall.

Where this comes from: every "Brian Breslin" match on Newspapers.com for Pennsylvania (the Inquirer, Daily News and the central-Pa. papers, 1987–1996) and every Breslin mention in the six digitized volumes of The Bucknellian, Bucknell's student paper, on the Internet Archive. The Northeast Times isn't digitized anywhere — the Free Library of Philadelphia keeps it on microfilm, so those box scores are still out there to add.
