IN THE PAPERS

What The Philadelphia Inquirer, the Daily News and the central Pennsylvania papers wrote about Brian, 1987–1994. Clippings from Newspapers.com.

Archbishop Ryan High School · 1987–1991

  • The Philadelphia InquirerNovember 17, 1987

    Freshman year: "The Raiders got a bit of good news in the form of Brian Breslin, who won the freshman race at the league championships."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerSeptember 20, 1988

    Sophomore year: on the soccer team and available to the cross country squad only on weekends. "Breslin was the league's freshman cross-country champ last year."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerNovember 17, 1988

    Soccer: Archbishop Ryan 2, Cardinal Dougherty 0 in the division semifinal. The Raiders went undefeated in the league.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerJanuary 31, 1989

    Indoor track: Marcello Rodriguez, Bill Pierce, Chris Pendergast and Brian Breslin win the two-mile relay in 8:37.3.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerMarch 13, 1989

    Catholic League indoor championships at Lehigh: Brian wins the two-mile in 9:54.9 as Ryan takes the team title.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerApril 4, 1989

    Spring preview: "sophomore Brian Breslin stepped in and helped the foursome run 7:59.5 at the Colonial Relays."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerMay 18, 1989

    Catholic League North championships: Ryan's 3,200 relay (Cook, Rodriguez, McFillin, Breslin) wins in 8:14.3.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerMay 24, 1989

    Catholic League champion in the 3,200 meters, 9:49.39: "kicked past Connolly to win by 40 yards." "It was a slow pace for the first mile, which was good for me."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerSeptember 19, 1989

    Brothers Jim and Brian return for Ryan cross country. "Brian Breslin is the league champ in the 2 mile, indoor and outdoor. Brian is a starter on Ryan's soccer team."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerSeptember 21, 1989

    "Ryan rolls to a 3-0 triumph in soccer": Brian Bradby and Brian Breslin add second-half goals against Cardinal Dougherty.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerSeptember 27, 1989

    Two goals in a 12-0 soccer win over Bishop McDevitt.

  • Philadelphia Daily NewsOctober 4, 1989

    "Archbishop Ryan 11, Bishop McDevitt 0: Brian Breslin and Brian Bradby each had hat tricks."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerOctober 10, 1989

    "Part-time star": within a tenth of a second of second place at the Salesianum Invitational. "Brian's beating everybody in the league this year," coach Ed Ulmer said.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerOctober 24, 1989

    "Ryan takes Manhattan": second place in 13:26 at the Eastern U.S. Championships, Van Cortlandt Park, with brother Jim 29th.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerNovember 7, 1989

    "Breslin to the rescue": a day after a soccer game, Brian runs the Northern Division championship. "Without Brian Breslin, we were getting dusted," Ulmer said.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerNovember 15, 1989

    "Running hurt": fifth in the Catholic League championship in 16:57, the best time of any Northeast runner, on a taped ankle twisted on a tree root.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerNovember 26, 1989

    Athlete of the Week. A double-overtime soccer match against Father Judge one day, fifth in the league cross country meet the next. Starting halfback, then right wing. 5-7, 140.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerNovember 26, 1989

    "That's the way I run all the time, since I'm always playing soccer." At the end of sophomore year he was ranked fifth academically in his class at Ryan.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerJanuary 7, 1990

    Indoor: Ryan's 3,200 relay (John Cooke, Jim Breslin, Brian Breslin, Bill Pierce) sets a meet record, 8:20.4.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerJanuary 21, 1990

    "Brothers from Archbishop Ryan win Coaches Association meet": Jim takes the mile and Brian the two-mile at St. Joseph's.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerJanuary 23, 1990

    "Two is better than one": "With a lap to go, I was second and started sprinting all-out. On the last turn, we were neck-and-neck, and I was just able to get him."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerFebruary 13, 1990

    Lehigh: Brian wins the two-mile in 9:49.9, moving to sixth in the area's top 10.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerFebruary 27, 1990

    "Ryan wins relay by a fraction of a second": Brian anchors the distance medley at the Meet of Champions, holding off Council Rock's Bob Hamer. "We knew we had to give Brian a lead," said his brother Jim.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerMarch 12, 1990

    Catholic League indoor championships: Brian wins the mile in 4:29.27, with Jim third. Ryan's 3,200 relay runs 8:07.2 at the Eastern championships at Princeton.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerMarch 27, 1990

    Spring preview: the distance medley of Jim Breslin, Ed Kelleher, John Cooke and Brian Breslin has the best time in the state, 10:32.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerApril 17, 1990

    School record in the 3,200 relay, breaking a mark from 1976. "Brian Breslin, a junior, ran the team's fastest split, 1:55.56, to secure the record."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerApril 22, 1990

    Father Judge Relays: Ryan (Jim Breslin, Jamie Silva, John Cooke, Brian Breslin) wins the 3,200 relay in 7:52.3.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerApril 28, 1990

    Penn Relays: sixth in the Championship of America distance medley. "Anchor Brian Breslin, the Catholic League indoor champion in the mile, accounted for a big chunk of the drop."

  • Philadelphia Daily NewsApril 28, 1990

    "Sixth for Ryan": Jim Breslin, Ed Kelleher, John Cooke and Brian Breslin in the boys' distance medley Championship of America.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerMay 1, 1990

    The season's best distance medley by a Philadelphia-area school, 10:17.3, the fastest American team but one. "Junior Brian Breslin ran a career-best in the 1,600-meter anchor leg."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerMay 6, 1990

    Bensalem Invitational: Brian wins the 1,600 in 4:20.6, breaking a meet record that had stood since 1983.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerMay 8, 1990

    Catholic League Northern Division champion in the mile, 4:25.3.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerMay 13, 1990

    Loucks Games, White Plains, N.Y., 166 schools from 10 states and Canada: second place in the 3,200.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerMay 15, 1990

    "Breslin breezes to win."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerMay 22, 1990

    Catholic League champions in the 3,200 relay: Jim Breslin, John Cooke, Jaime Silva and Brian Breslin, 7:57.63.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerSeptember 11, 1990

    Senior year soccer preview: "Brian Breslin, who hopes to compete in the Catholic League cross-country championships this fall, will provide the speed at the forward spot."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerSeptember 12, 1990

    "Archbishop Ryan, behind Brian Breslin's hat trick, rolled to a 6-1 triumph at Bishop McDevitt."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerSeptember 18, 1990

    Cross country preview, Ed Ulmer's 25th season: "But any loss is easily erased by Brian Breslin. 'He's definitely our top runner.'"

  • The Philadelphia InquirerSeptember 25, 1990

    "Ryan, La Salle play to a 2-2 tie": brought down in the box in the 41st minute; "Midfielders Breslin and Rogers fueled the surge."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerOctober 9, 1990

    With no soccer game that day, Brian runs: second overall in 16:38 at Belmont Plateau.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerOctober 16, 1990

    "North Catholic's Sal Celebre battles Ryan's Brian Breslin." Photo by Ed Hille.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerOctober 20, 1990

    Ryan 3, La Salle 1: "Brian Toff, Paul Zgalich and Brian Breslin scored for the Raiders."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerOctober 30, 1990

    Ryan Invitational: fifth of 20 teams' runners in 16:40. Midweek, first in 16:57, running the last mile and a half alone.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerNovember 1, 1990

    Catholic League Northern Division championship: third in 16:41.

  • Philadelphia Daily NewsNovember 12, 1990

    Catholic League championship: "Mike McClafferty held off a game comeback by Archbishop Ryan's Brian Breslin to earn the individual crown." Brian second in 16:39.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerNovember 13, 1990

    "Ryan's Brian Breslin comes out of the woods 2d at Belmont."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerNovember 20, 1990

    "He likes soccer and running ... and excels in both of them." First-team all-Catholic in cross country and in soccer, the same fall. "I'm not sure anyone has ever done what Brian did this year."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerNovember 20, 1990

    "He's a remarkably dedicated young man." He began playing soccer in second grade. "When the season began and I thought about how much I love soccer and how long I'd been playing it, I just couldn't allow myself not to play."

  • The Philadelphia InquirerDecember 18, 1990

    Indoor: Brian hands off with "a seemingly insurmountable eight-second lead." "I knew he was the guy to beat," said Haverford's Cromwell.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerJanuary 8, 1991

    Ryan's distance medley, fifth at the Penn Relays and the second-fastest American team, returns three of four. Jim is now running for East Stroudsburg.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerJanuary 17, 1991

    Lawrenceville Relays: Ryan's distance medley (Silva, Turner, Cooke, Breslin) runs 10:41.1, the area's fastest of the season.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerFebruary 24, 1991

    Meet of Champions at Lehigh: Brian wins the 1,000 in 2:17.8.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerMarch 4, 1991

    Pennsylvania Coaches state meet: third in the mile, 4:25.50; Ryan wins the two-mile relay in 8:07.45.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerMarch 10, 1991

    Catholic League indoor championships: second in the 1,000 by three hundredths of a second, and second in the mile.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerApril 6, 1991

    "Brian Breslin anchored Archbishop Ryan's 3,200-meter relay team to first place in the Colonial Relays at William & Mary," 7:50.9.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerApril 21, 1991

    Father Judge Relays: Silva, Higgins, Cooke and Breslin win the 3,200 relay in 7:54.6.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerMay 5, 1991

    Bensalem Invitational: Brian defends his 1,600 title in 4:24.1.

  • The Philadelphia InquirerMay 23, 1991

    Catholic League championships at La Salle: "Leading for most of the race, Scully beat off a last second charge by Breslin and won the mile in 4:24.75."

Bucknell University · 1991–1996

  • The Daily Item, SunburyFebruary 4, 1992

    Freshman year: Bucknell's distance medley (Brian Breslin, Kristofer Fischlein, Tom Eagleson, Jeff Loomis) qualifies for the IC4A championships at the Princeton Relays, 10:06.6.

  • The Patriot-News, HarrisburgOctober 25, 1992

    Bucknell soccer: second-half goals by Brian Breslin.

  • Public Opinion, ChambersburgOctober 26, 1992

    Bucknell 3, Shippensburg 1: "Bucknell broke the tie in the second half when Brian Breslin..."

  • Philadelphia Daily NewsDecember 31, 1992

    "Bucknell's best": sophomore Brian Breslin, second in the 800 at the Patriot League indoor championships.

  • The Morning Call, AllentownFebruary 28, 1993

    Patriot League indoor championships at Bucknell: second in the 1,000.

  • The Sentinel, CarlisleNovember 4, 1993

    Bucknell soccer: an assist at 54 minutes and a goal at 70 in a 4-0 win over Shippensburg.

  • The Daily Item, SunburySeptember 4, 1994

    Senior year at Bucknell: eight starters back from an 11-5-3 team. "Greg Beatty, Rich Shaheen, Tom Roller and Brian Breslin will lead the attack for the Bison."
